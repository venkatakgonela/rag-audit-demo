# Synthetic Drying visits

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Drying diary

Arrange the first drying review within 5 working days of the initial visit. This service target applies to all editions.

## 2.1 Working instructions

Before moving equipment, photograph its settings. If access has to be postponed, record the reason and offer two replacement appointments. An unchanged reading should be recorded rather than copied from a previous sheet.
