Synthetic Voyage, edition 3. Only this edition is described here.

## 1.1 Meaning and exclusions

This version covers declared fragile effects only when the packing schedule names them. A declared set is valued item by item; loss of one piece does not automatically establish loss of the entire set.

## 2.1 Required action

The packing schedule must identify each fragile item with a separate row and a condition photograph.
