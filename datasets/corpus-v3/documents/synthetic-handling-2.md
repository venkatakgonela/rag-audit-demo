# Synthetic Fire evidence

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Safe collection

A fire report uses Form Ember-4 and must separate witnessed events from later assumptions.

## 2.1 Working instructions

Never enter an unsafe room to collect evidence. An assessor can accept an account from outside the building. Keep a list of missing documents rather than recreating them as if they were originals.
