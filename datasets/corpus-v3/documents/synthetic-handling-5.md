# Synthetic Lost receipts

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Alternative proof

Where a receipt is lost, a dated bank entry and a supplier duplicate can be considered together.

## 2.1 Working instructions

A bank entry alone may not identify the item. State what each substitute document establishes and what remains uncertain. Do not edit an old duplicate to look like a new purchase.
