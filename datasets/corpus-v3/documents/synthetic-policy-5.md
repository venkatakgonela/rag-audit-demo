# Synthetic Workshop edition 3 wording

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Product and edition

Synthetic Workshop, edition 3. Only this edition is described here.

## 1.1 Meaning and exclusions

Portable tooling is assessed separately from fixed equipment. A maintenance defect is not itself insured, although resulting sudden fire damage may be. Replacement with a higher-capacity machine is limited to the equivalent repair basis.

## 2.1 Required action

An item above GBP 850.00 requires both a repair estimate and an equivalent replacement estimate.

## 3.1 Calculation terms

Currency: GBP.
Excess: GBP 225.00.
Limit: GBP 4500.00.
Covered perils: water, fire.
Notification window: 19 days.
