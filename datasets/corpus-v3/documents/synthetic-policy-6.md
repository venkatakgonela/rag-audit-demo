Synthetic Voyage, edition 1. Only this edition is described here.

## 1.1 Meaning and exclusions

Personal effects must travel with the declared household. Unattended goods in an unlocked vehicle are excluded. A journey begins when the consignment leaves the declared collection point, not when packing starts.

## 2.1 Required action

Report broken seals using Form Wren-1 within 4 days of delivery.
