Synthetic Orchard, edition 2. Only this edition is described here.

## 1.1 Meaning and exclusions

A temporary cover can limit rain entry after a covered fire, but upgrading the roof is not an emergency expense. The insured must preserve safe access for assessment and should not climb an unstable structure.

## 2.1 Required action

Record the temporary cover dimensions and contractor attendance on Form Fern-2.
