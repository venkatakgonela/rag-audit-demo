# Synthetic Salvage handling

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Storage steps

Keep separated salvage for 9 days after written disposal instructions unless a safety hazard requires earlier removal.

## 2.1 Working instructions

Mark the storage area without putting a full claim reference on a publicly visible container. Record quantity and condition at removal. Unsafe material should not be retained solely to satisfy an inspection preference.
