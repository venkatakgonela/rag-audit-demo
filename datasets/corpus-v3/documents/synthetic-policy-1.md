# Synthetic Hearth edition 2 wording

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Product and edition

Synthetic Hearth, edition 2. Only this edition is described here.

## 1.1 Meaning and exclusions

Water released suddenly from a fixed domestic appliance is included as escape of water. Slow seepage from failed sealant is excluded. This edition adds reasonable access work beneath a fitted floor, but not matching undamaged flooring in another room.

## 2.1 Required action

A plumber must identify the failed component on Form Reed-2 before the concealed access work begins.
