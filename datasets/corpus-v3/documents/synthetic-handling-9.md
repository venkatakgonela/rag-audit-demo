# Synthetic Payment reconciliation

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Duplicate transfers

Investigate a possible duplicate transfer before requesting repayment; allow 7 working days for ledger reconciliation.

## 2.1 Working instructions

Compare the transaction reference and destination with the authorised instruction. A duplicate notification email is not evidence that money moved twice. Keep the correction record with the original entry.
