# Synthetic Decision review

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Escalation

A disputed handling decision can be referred to the review desk within 30 calendar days of its letter.

## 2.1 Working instructions

The reviewer must not be the original decision maker. Provide the disputed sentence and the evidence believed to change it; the request is not an invitation to resubmit unrelated documents.
