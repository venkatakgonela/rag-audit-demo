# Synthetic Handover checklist

SYNTHETIC — Synthetic Lanternfold Mutual. Fictional demonstration only.

## Scope

This handling procedure applies to all four synthetic products and all three editions. Policy terms still govern cover.

## Ownership transfer

A handler transferring a file must identify the next action, its due date and the person responsible for confirming completion.

## 2.1 Working instructions

The receiving handler acknowledges the transfer before the old task is closed. A handover does not reset a policy notification window. Outstanding safety concerns remain visible at the top of the file.
